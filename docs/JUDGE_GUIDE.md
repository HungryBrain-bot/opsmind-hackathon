# OpsMind Judge Guide

## 60-second demo

1. Open the OpsMind workspace.
2. Keep **Certificate expiry · HF-PROD-02** selected.
3. Select **Start golden investigation**.
4. Watch the phase rail move through planning, evidence collection, reasoning, resolution, verification and knowledge capture.
5. Select an evidence node in the graph to inspect its source and detail.
6. Compare the leading and rejected hypotheses.
7. Select **Replay** to animate how the conclusion was reached.
8. Switch to **Executive view** to show the same investigation as a concise operational decision.

## What to notice

- OpsMind does not produce a one-shot answer.
- Confidence rises only after corroborated evidence is collected.
- Contradicting evidence rejects plausible alternatives.
- Resolution actions carry rationale, risk, evidence references and verification criteria.
- The final incident pattern is captured as reusable operational memory.
